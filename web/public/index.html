Reproducible inputs.

usdata is for anyone who puts federal data, from NOAA weather to EPA air quality, into a paper, a model, or a report and has to show where every number came from. One command finds the dataset, one fetches the exact source file, one opens it, and a small manifest lets anyone repeat the download and prove the bytes match.

YOUR TERMINAL 
python -m pip install "usdata[pandas]"
usdata search precipitation --location Oklahoma
usdata fetch noaa:ghcn-daily -p stations=USW00013967 --start 2024-05-06 --end 2024-05-07 --vars PRCP,TMAX
usdata pull dataset.yaml && usdata verify dataset.yaml

ONE RESULT, END TO END

How high was Hurricane Helene's storm surge at Cedar Key?

Three fetches: six-minute water levels and tide predictions for one NOAA gauge, and the hurricane's best track. Subtracting the predictions from the observations isolates the surge.
