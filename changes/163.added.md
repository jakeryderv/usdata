`usdata datasets` lists the curated registry as a table of id, status, domain, formats, reader, and summary, filtered by `--provider`, `--domain`, `--format`, `--reader`, `--capability`, and `--status`; `usdata search` takes the same filters, both accept `--json` for a machine-readable array, and the SDK gains `usdata.datasets()` and the matching keyword arguments on `Registry.list` and `Registry.search`.
