`epa:aqs-daily` and `usgs:earthquakes` now send bounding-box edges to six decimal places, about 0.1 m, instead of six significant digits, which could move an edge such as -105.123456 by tens of metres. An AQS box request's URL now matches its asset id: two boxes share one exactly when they share the other. Edges given to six decimals or fewer are sent as before, so the committed wildfire-smoke lockfile's href is unchanged; a finer box, such as one derived from `--lat`/`--lon` or a place envelope, gets a new href, and for ComCat a new asset id, on its next resolve.
