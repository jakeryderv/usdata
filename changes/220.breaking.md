A date alone as a query, CLI, or manifest `end` now means the last instant of that UTC day rather than its first, so `--start 2024-05-07 --end 2024-05-07` is the whole day: listing-based sources such as GOES, GLM, MRMS, HRRR, and GFS gain the rest of the day, CO-OPS reads it as 23:59, and calendar-date sources are unchanged. Window limits are measured between instants, so two bare dates a day apart now span two days.
