Two datasets from the Iowa Environmental Mesonet archive: `noaa:nws-warnings` delivers every NWS watch, warning, and advisory with its geometry (storm-based polygons and the counties and zones each product named), and `noaa:spc-outlooks` delivers SPC convective and fire weather outlooks selected by issuance time. Both arrive as zipped shapefiles, one per UTC month, rewritten canonically so lockfiles can pin them.
