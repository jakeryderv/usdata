`noaa:nws-vtec-events` fetches the National Weather Service watches, warnings, and advisories issued for one county, as CSV from the Iowa Environmental Mesonet's archive, which is the maintained one since the NWS API keeps none. `--location "Osage County, OK"` selects that county exactly and a bare `bbox` is refused; `ugc` names a code explicitly, which is the only way to reach a forecast zone, and `phenomena` with `significance` narrows to one event type. A window selects events by when they were issued, not by when they were in effect, and the guide gives the recipe for the second question.
