The `noaa:nexrad-level2` and `noaa:nexrad-level3` catalog extents now reach east to 27°W, so searching at the Lajes radar in the Azores (`LPLA`) finds them. The Guam, Korea, and Okinawa radars (`PGUA`, `RKJK`, `RKSG`, `RODN`) lie beyond the antimeridian, which a single extent cannot wrap across; the NEXRAD guide and catalog descriptions now say so, and those radars remain available by site id.
