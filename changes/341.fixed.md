`location="Alaska"` and `location="Aleutians West Census Area, AK"` now resolve to their western-hemisphere part, not a box spanning 359 degrees of longitude that sent adapters a band around the whole globe and returned earthquakes in Kamchatka and Iceland. The Near Islands and part of the Rat Islands, which lie in the eastern hemisphere, are left out; pass a `bbox` for them. The place generator refuses to write any box that crosses the antimeridian, and the bundled `places.sources.json` records each clipped place and the box it dropped.
