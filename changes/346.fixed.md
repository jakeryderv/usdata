NBM probability-threshold messages can now be selected: a `messages` selector's further text is everything after the step text, colons included, so `APCP:surface:0-1 hour acc fcst:prob >0.254:prob fcst 255/255` selects that threshold and every index line reads back as a selector for itself. A selector whose short name and level match but whose step or further text does not now lists the steps or further texts published there.
